## 3. One thing to decide: "Is it Regulatory" doing two jobs

You asked to use "Is it Regulatory" in place of Management Attention. I have done that. The consequence is that one Yes or No field now carries two meanings:

- this work is regulatory, and
- this work needs management attention.

In the mock-up, two Initiatives have it set to Yes: I1 (which really is regulatory) and I3 (which is Red and needs attention but is not regulatory). So:

- The panel "Regulatory and Red" on dashboard 1 returns C2, I1 and I3.
- The panel "regulatory Initiatives" on dashboard 2 returns I1 and I3, although only I1 is regulatory.

That is workable for a mock-up. For real use it has a cost: anyone reading the field, including audit, will take "Is it Regulatory = Yes" at its word.

An alternative that needs no admin change: keep "Is it Regulatory" for its real meaning, and mark "needs management attention" with a **label**, `mgmt-attention`. The one filter that changes is:

```text
filter = "MOCK: all" AND issuetype in (Initiative, Capability) AND status not in (Released, Done)
AND (labels = mgmt-attention OR "RAG Status" = Red)
```

Say if you prefer that and I will regenerate the files with it.

## 4. What you lose with these fields, and what to do

| Lost | Effect | Workaround |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The kind of enabler | Investment mix shows only enabler against not enabler | Enough to start; use a label if a finer split is wanted |
| The four scores | Jira cannot show why a score is what it is | Keep the four numbers in the Initiative's description, or on the scoring page in Confluence |
| A separate date for recovery | "Date to Green" cannot be sorted or filtered | Write the date at the start of RAG Description: "By 15 Jan: ..." |
| A dedicated Impact field | Priority also drives other things in Jira, such as ordering | Acceptable for Delivery RAID tickets, which are not worked from a backlog |
